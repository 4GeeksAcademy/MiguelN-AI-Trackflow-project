# Project Brief

## Business description

TrackFlow Inc. is a last-mile delivery and warehouse management company founded in 2009, with operations in Mexico and Spain with warehouses in Monterrey and Zaragoza. The company serves e-commerce brands by managing the full logistics lifecycle: inventory storage, pick-and-pack, carrier dispatch, delivery tracking, and returns handling. With around 130 employees and annual revenue near EUR 9M, TrackFlow combines physical operations teams and a growing technology unit to support reliable, cross-border logistics execution.

Its core business value is helping brands outsource logistics complexity so they can focus on product and growth. This includes coordinating multiple carriers, managing high return volumes, and maintaining service quality for both B2B clients and end consumers.

## Project objectives

1. Build a unified operational backbone across Mexico and Spain, including shared inventory visibility and consistent data flows between warehouse, carrier, returns, and customer service systems.
2. Reduce manual work by automating repetitive workflows such as order ingestion, carrier selection, tracking updates, return approvals, and weekly executive reporting.
3. Improve decision-making speed and quality by providing real-time dashboards for operations, commercial teams, and leadership.
4. Increase service quality for clients and recipients through faster issue resolution, proactive alerts, and an always-available support experience.
5. Strengthen scalability and resilience by modernizing integrations, adding monitoring/telemetry, and standardizing core logistics processes.

## What problem it solves

TrackFlow currently operates with fragmented systems, manual processes, and limited real-time visibility. The two warehouses do not share a single source of truth for inventory, carrier operations are managed with heavy manual coordination, returns require case-by-case human review, and customer support handles high volumes of repetitive inquiries without unified tooling. Leadership reporting is also assembled manually, producing delayed and sometimes outdated data.

This project solves those issues by creating an integrated, data-driven logistics platform with automation and AI-enabled support. The result is fewer operational errors, faster response times, lower administrative overhead, and better business decisions based on current, reliable information.
